Shipping & returns

Shipping

Shipping is free to all 50 US states. Orders are prepared within 1–2 business days at our partner warehouse in the United States and usually arrive in {{shipping.minDays}}–{{shipping.maxDays}} business days. You'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.

We currently ship to US addresses only (no APO/FPO or international addresses).

{{shipping.returnDays}}-day money-back guarantee

If you're not happy with your Snowlit, email within {{shipping.returnDays}} days of delivery with the email you used at checkout. We'll refund you to your original payment method. For most orders we don't even ask you to send it back.

Damaged or missing parts

If something arrives broken or a part is missing, send us a photo within 14 days of delivery and we'll send a replacement or refund you, your choice.
